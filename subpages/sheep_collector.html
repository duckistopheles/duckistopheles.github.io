Sheep Collector

A solo developed Prototyp for a cozy Puzzle Adventure build in Unity. Collect Sheep and other stuff with your Ufo and Shoot them to solve puzzles. Playable in browser on itch.io

For this project I did:

  • Programming
  • Game and Levedesign
  • Shader for Beam, Water and Objects hidden by environment
  • 3D Modeling and Texturing of the Sheeps, the Wolves and the UFO
